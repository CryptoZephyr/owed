## One role, one promise

An employer sets an amount for each candidate, the number of places, and a reply date. The total hold covers every place.

## Follow the steps

1. The employer approves one PayPal hold for the role.
2. The employer sends each interviewed candidate a private invitation.
3. Each candidate signs in with the invited PayPal account and accepts the promise.
4. The employer sends each candidate a status before the date.
5. If no status arrives by the deadline, Owed charges that candidate's amount and pays it through PayPal.
6. When the role is ready to close, the employer closes it and PayPal releases what remains.

## When the employer sends a status

The employer can say the candidate is moving forward, not moving forward, or that the team is still deciding with one later date. See [how closure messages work](/docs/guides/send-closure).

## When no status arrives

Owed follows the saved deadline and payment rules. See [what happens at the deadline](/docs/concepts/what-happens-at-the-deadline).

## What this flow proves

The page records each step Owed knows about. PayPal's own records show whether it approved or moved money.
