## Before you start

Use PayPal sandbox test accounts, not personal PayPal accounts. All money in the live demo is test money.

## Try the employer path

Sign in with a sandbox account, create a role, and approve its hold in PayPal. Then invite a candidate using the email for that candidate's sandbox account.

## Try the candidate path

Open the private invitation and sign in with the invited sandbox account. Check the amount and date before accepting.

## What the try-out proves

A successful run shows that the tested account and flow worked at that time. It doesn't show that live PayPal is ready or that every account will work.

## Where to look next

The [employer guide](/docs/guides/create-a-role) explains how to set up a promise. The [candidate guide](/docs/guides/claim-a-guarantee) explains how to accept one.
