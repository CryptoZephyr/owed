## “This invitation can't be opened with this PayPal account”

The link may have been replaced or sent to another account. Sign out and use the invited account, or ask the employer for the latest link.

## “PayPal login was canceled or expired”

Start sign-in again with a PayPal sandbox test account. A personal account can't sign in to the sandbox.

## “AI drafting didn't respond”

Owed fills in its standard message instead. You can send it, edit it, or try again later. A previous request reached the free writing service's daily cap. Deployed drafting with the current model hasn't been verified.

## A field needs attention

Follow the message beside the field. For example, choose a reply date within 21 days and leave at least one day before the hold expires.

## A role or payment needs review

Don't create another role or try to pay the candidate yourself. Keep the role and PayPal reference, wait for Owed to check, and contact the operator if it remains under review.
