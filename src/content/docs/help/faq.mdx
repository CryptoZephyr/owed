## Who is Owed for?

Employers who interview candidates and candidates who want a clear reply date. See [the introduction](/docs/get-started/introduction).

## Does Owed use real money?

The live demo uses PayPal sandbox accounts and test money. See [what is supported](/docs/reference/supported).

## What if the employer doesn't reply?

Owed follows the saved deadline and payment rules. See [what happens at the deadline](/docs/concepts/what-happens-at-the-deadline).

## Does AI decide who gets paid?

No. AI helps with message wording. See [AI and emails](/docs/concepts/ai-and-emails).

## Who pays PayPal fees?

Owed absorbs the fees today. See [fees and limits](/docs/reference/fees-and-limits).
