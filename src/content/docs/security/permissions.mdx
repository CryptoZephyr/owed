## The employer approves the hold

The hold belongs to one role and its total amount. The employer must approve it in PayPal before Owed enables candidate invitations.

## The candidate accepts the invitation

The private link alone isn't enough. Owed checks the signed-in candidate's verified PayPal email against the intended email, then binds the promise to the account that accepts.

## Each action stays in its lane

The employer sends closure and can close the role when every candidate is settled. Owed's fixed rules decide when to charge, pay, or release money.

## What this protects

Owed checks role ownership and candidate identity before accepting an action. The [trust model](/docs/security/trust-model) explains which records confirm the result.
