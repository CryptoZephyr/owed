## PayPal confirms identity and money movement

PayPal handles sign-in, the role hold, candidate payments, payouts, and releases. Its own records are the source for what happened to money.

## Owed applies the promise

Owed checks the role, candidate, amount, and deadline before it asks PayPal to act. It keeps the promise and the result PayPal returns.

## What people can check

The employer and candidate pages show the promise and its status. A page or screenshot can show what Owed recorded, but PayPal's transaction record proves what PayPal did.
