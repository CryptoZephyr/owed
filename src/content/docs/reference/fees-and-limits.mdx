## Amounts and places

Each candidate's guarantee can be $1 to $1,000. A role can hold 1 to 100 places. The total hold is the guarantee per candidate multiplied by the number of places.

## Dates and currency

The reply date must be within 21 days and leave at least one day before the PayPal hold expires. Owed currently uses US dollars for holds, charges, and payouts.

## PayPal fees

The hold covers candidate guarantees. Owed absorbs PayPal fees, and candidates receive the full promised amount. One recorded $1.00 sandbox capture carried a $0.52 PayPal fee. That result applies to that transaction, not every payment.

## For the curious

The recorded fee appears in the sandbox results in the README and `paypal-plan.md`. There is no separate employer fee amount in the current hold.
