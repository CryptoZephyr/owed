## Sandbox only

Owed has only been verified with PayPal sandbox accounts and test money. Live PayPal and real employer use haven't been tested.

## PayPal webhooks are not wired

Owed doesn't receive PayPal webhook events. It checks PayPal records through its scheduled work.

## AI and email limits

An email send record doesn't prove delivery to the inbox. AI drafting depends on a free writing service with a daily cap, so it can be unavailable for the rest of a day.

## Other boundaries

There is no product-wide pause switch. An unclaimed payout stays unclaimed, with no automatic charity fallback. Real employer demand has not been tested.
