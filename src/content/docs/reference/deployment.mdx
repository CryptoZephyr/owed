## Hosted demo

Open the [Owed demo](https://owed-vert-ten.vercel.app). It uses PayPal sandbox accounts and test money.

## Health check

The health check reports whether the app answered and which PayPal environment is configured at that moment.

## What it cannot prove

A health response doesn't prove that sign-in, email, AI drafting, or a payment works. It also doesn't promise uptime or production support.
