## Where to find the demo

Open the [hosted Owed demo](https://owed-vert-ten.vercel.app). It is configured for the PayPal sandbox.

## What a health response proves

The health check shows that the app answered and reports which PayPal environment it is configured to use at that time.

## What it doesn't prove

A health response doesn't prove that sign-in, email, AI drafting, or a PayPal payment works. It also doesn't prove that every user flow is available.
