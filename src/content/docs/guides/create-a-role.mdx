## What you see

The role form asks for a title, employer name, amount for each candidate, number of places, and reply date. A receipt shows the total hold.

## What you do

Enter the promise details and choose a reply date within 21 days. Check the receipt, then choose “Create role.”

## What Owed does

Owed multiplies the amount per candidate by the number of places. It saves the role and gives you the option to approve one hold in PayPal.

## How you know it worked

The role appears on your employer page, with its total hold and a prompt to fund it. The hold starts only after you approve it in PayPal. See [how the hold works](/docs/concepts/how-the-money-is-held).

## If nothing changes

Read the field message and correct the item it names. The form keeps the values you entered. Amounts, place counts, and date limits are listed in [Fees and limits](/docs/reference/fees-and-limits).
