## What you see

Each open candidate has a form for a status: moving forward, not moving forward, or still deciding.

## What you do

Choose the status and send Owed's standard message, or ask for a draft and review it before sending. A still-deciding answer needs one later reply date.

## What Owed does

Owed records the status and message, shows them to the candidate, and sends an email when an address is available. A warning may ask you to review or acknowledge wording.

## How you know it worked

The role page shows “Message sent” and the message. The candidate can see the status on their page. See [AI and emails](/docs/concepts/ai-and-emails) for what the writing help and notices do.

## If nothing changes

Read the message under the form. If the deadline has passed, closure can't cancel a payment already due. See [what happens at the deadline](/docs/concepts/what-happens-at-the-deadline).
