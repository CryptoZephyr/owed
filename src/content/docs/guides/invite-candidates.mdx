## What you see

An active role shows how many funded places remain and has a form for a candidate name and PayPal email.

## What you do

Enter the name and the verified PayPal email for the account the candidate should use. Create the invitation, copy its private link, and send it directly to that candidate.

## What Owed does

Owed saves the invitation details and shows the link once. Only funded places can be used, and each PayPal email can be invited once for the role.

## How you know it worked

The candidate appears on the role page as not accepted yet. Their invitation stays tied to the intended account. See [sign-in and identity](/docs/concepts/sign-in-and-identity).

## If nothing changes

Check that the role has an approved hold and an available place. Before the deadline, you can correct an unclaimed invitation's email. Owed replaces the link, so send the new one.
