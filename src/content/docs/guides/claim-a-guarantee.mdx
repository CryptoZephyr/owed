## What you see

The private invitation shows the role, employer, promised amount, and reply date before you accept.

## What you do

Sign in with the verified PayPal account the employer invited. Check the details, then accept the promise.

## What Owed does

Owed checks that your verified PayPal email matches the invitation. After you accept, the promise appears on your candidate page.

## How you know it worked

The candidate page shows the promise and its reply date. If no status arrives by the deadline, Owed pays the amount to the PayPal account that accepted the invitation.

## If nothing changes

If the signed-in account doesn't match, sign out and use the invited account. See [sign-in and identity](/docs/concepts/sign-in-and-identity) for how matching works.
