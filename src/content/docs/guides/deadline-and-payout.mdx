## What you see

The role page shows each candidate's reply date and the current payment status.

## What you do

Send each candidate a status before the date. If a date passes, allow Owed to check the PayPal result before taking another action.

## What Owed does

Owed sends a reminder when a reply is due within 24 hours. After an unanswered deadline, it charges the candidate's amount from the role hold and sends that amount to the PayPal account that accepted the invitation.

## How you know it worked

The role page shows the payment progress. PayPal can report a payout as pending or unclaimed before it is complete. See [the deadline rules](/docs/concepts/what-happens-at-the-deadline).

## If nothing changes

Don't try to pay the candidate yourself or create another role. Keep the role and PayPal reference, and contact the operator if the page says the role needs review.
