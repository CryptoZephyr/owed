## What you see

An active role shows a “Close role and release the rest” action.

## What you do

Send each candidate a status and let any owed payments settle. Then close the role.

## What Owed does

Owed checks that no candidate still needs a status or payment. It asks PayPal to release the unused part of the hold.

## How you know it worked

The role closes after PayPal confirms the release. The role page shows its final amounts, and PayPal's record shows what happened to the hold.

## If nothing changes

The role page explains which candidate still needs attention. Resolve that item first. For unclear payment outcomes, follow [Recovery](/docs/guides/recovery).
