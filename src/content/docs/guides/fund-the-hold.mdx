## What you see

The role page shows the amount to approve and opens PayPal's sandbox approval page.

## What you do

Check the amount and role description in PayPal. Approve the hold with the employer's sandbox account, then return to Owed.

## What Owed does

Owed waits for PayPal to confirm the hold. Candidate invitations become available after approval.

## How you know it worked

The role shows an active hold and lets you invite candidates. PayPal's record is the source for whether it approved the hold. See [how the money is held](/docs/concepts/how-the-money-is-held).

## If nothing changes

Return to the role page and refresh. If approval remains pending or the role needs review, don't create a second role. Ask the operator to check the role and PayPal record.
