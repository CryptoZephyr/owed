## What you see

A role or payment may show that PayPal is still being checked or that the item needs review.

## What you do

Wait for the role page to refresh. Keep the role and PayPal reference. If it still needs review, contact the Owed operator.

## What Owed does

Owed checks PayPal before retrying an uncertain money action. It stops for review when it can't safely confirm the result. See [Failure and recovery](/docs/security/failure-and-recovery).

## How you know it worked

The role page shows the latest status after PayPal confirms it. PayPal's own record is the source for whether money moved.

## If nothing changes

Don't create another role, send a separate payment, or assume a timeout means the action failed. Give the operator the role and PayPal reference.
