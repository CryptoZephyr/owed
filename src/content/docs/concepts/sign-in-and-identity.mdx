## Sign in with PayPal

Employers and candidates use PayPal to sign in. Owed checks that the account is verified before it can fund a role or accept a guarantee.

## Match an invitation

The employer enters the PayPal email the candidate should use. Before acceptance, Owed checks that the signed-in account has that verified email. A different account can't claim the invitation.

## Keep the accepted account

After acceptance, Owed links the guarantee to that PayPal account. Any later payout goes to the account that accepted, not to an email address typed into the invitation.

## What you can check

The invitation shows the promise before acceptance. The candidate page shows it afterward. PayPal's sign-in record confirms which account accepted.
