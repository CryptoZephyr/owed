## AI helps with wording

An employer can ask for a short closure draft, edit it, and decide whether to send it. AI can flag wording that needs review. The employer remains responsible for the message.

## The message states a status

Closure must say whether a candidate is moving forward, not moving forward, or still deciding. AI may flag reasons or other risky wording. The employer can review the flags and acknowledge them before sending the unchanged message. A still-deciding message must include one later date, which can be set once.

## Emails follow saved events

Owed sends invitations, deadline reminders, closure messages, and payment notices when a usable email address is available. A sent record doesn't prove that the email reached the inbox.

## What AI does not control

AI only writes or flags words. Fixed rules decide whether Owed asks PayPal to hold, charge, pay, or release money. Deployed drafting with the current model hasn't been verified.

## For the curious

If a message is definitely not sent, Owed makes up to three attempts, with waits of five and ten minutes between retries. Unclear send results aren't retried automatically.
