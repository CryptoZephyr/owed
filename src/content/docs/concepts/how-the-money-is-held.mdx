## One hold for the role

The employer approves one PayPal hold for the role. Its total is the amount promised to each candidate multiplied by the number of places.

## The hold is not a charge

PayPal sets the money aside after approval. Owed charges a candidate's amount only when their reply date passes without closure.

## What happens to the rest

Each charge reduces the amount left in the hold. When every candidate has closure and any owed payments are settled, the employer can close the role and PayPal releases what remains.

## What proves the movement

The role page shows the hold and payment references. PayPal's own records show whether it approved, charged, or released money.
